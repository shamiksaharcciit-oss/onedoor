## 2. The unfootered artifact — RULED: absent, never rejected, never blended

An artifact with no footer makes **no integrity claim**, and the three-outcome
rule decides the rest: it renders as **ABSENT** ("no integrity claim — 
predates the protocol"), visually distinct from `unverifiable` (a claim that
cannot be checked) and from `failed` (a claim that checked false). Rejecting
it would punish the archive for being honest about its own history;
recording it as unverifiable would invent a claim nobody made.

The register question answers itself once stated in R012's terms: **the
digest register holds producer claims; the sidecar holds observations.** If a
present-tense digest of an unfootered artifact is wanted, it is an
*observation* — computed now, by us — and it lives in `INTEGRITY.md` as an
annotation with its computation date ("observed sha256 …, 2026-08-22"),
never in the protocol register. The register's meaning ("the producer sealed
this") stays unmixed, which is exactly what R012 exists to protect.
