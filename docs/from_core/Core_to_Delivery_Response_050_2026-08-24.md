## 4. The §6 finding — ruled

The document was written, the derivation is pure over its declared inputs, and the
`view that cites` ruling holds. The finding delivery brought back is nonetheless real,
and it resolves cleanly once the two questions are separated.

**The defect is a name, and the name is `exercised_effects`.** That word claims history.
The computation is a projection: *under the policy set being mapped, the observed traffic
would exercise these effects.* For a **candidate** — the Studio's actual purpose — that
projection is not a compromise, it is the correct and only sensible question: *if I
ratify this, what does it reach?* The map answers it exactly. For the **active** set, a
reader will read the same word as a historical record, and it is not one.

**Rename it.** `would_exercise`, `reachable_effects`, or delivery's better word — a name
that says projection rather than record. Naming-honesty is settled programme law and this
is its cheapest possible application: no migration, no preimage version, no column, and
the misreading closes. The footer keeps stating the limitation; the field stops inviting
it in the first place.

**And the historical question is not the coverage map's to answer.** Establishing which
effects actually resolved for a past row means taking that row's own `policy_version`,
loading the snapshot in force then, and resolving effects against the row's **frozen
params** — because `param_effects` means effects depend on params, so no join and no
column short of the engine's own resolution can settle it. That is the engine, run over
history, against sealed inputs. **That is a backtest** — S1's machinery, and precisely
why a backtest gets a receipt while a map gets a citation.

So: **no new hashed column, no `/3`, no migration.** The gap is not in the ledger; it is
the boundary between two products, and this finding confirms the boundary rather than
challenging it. The map projects and cites; the backtest measures and receipts. If a
deployer ever needs *"which effects did this range actually exercise"*, the honest answer
is to run a backtest over that range, not to make the map pretend.

Record the ruling in §6 in place of the open question, so the next reader finds the
answer rather than the deliberation.
