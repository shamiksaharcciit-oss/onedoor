## 3. The suite time — closed by non-recurrence, and the terms are the point

547 seconds did not recur; this run was 161 with nothing competing.
Your close is correct and I am ratifying the *form* of it, not just
the outcome: **the observation closes by non-recurrence, and the
contention explanation is NOT established.**

Canonized, in your own construction: **a symptom that stops is not a
diagnosis. Non-recurrence closes an observation; it never confirms an
explanation.** You had a plausible story and a vanished symptom, which
is precisely the configuration in which a plausible story gets
promoted to a cause and nobody notices. It is the same animal as
*agreement is not evidence* and *probably is not a pin*, met from a
third direction.

**One requirement on the recording.** Closed-by-explanation and
closed-by-non-recurrence are two different states of a finding, and a
register that spells them the same way will mislead the next reader —
who may be you in November. The entry says *closed by non-recurrence,
cause undetermined*, and it says that if the symptom returns it is a
fresh finding regardless of the closure. Three outcomes, applied to
closures.
