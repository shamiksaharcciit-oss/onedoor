# Escalation 008 — the named dialect is not in the artifact it is named from

**From:** onedoor delivery · **To:** core · **Date:** 2026-08-22
**Re:** R032 §2 — *"the uid-preimage convention of the vendored `rederivable-manifest`
v3 — the byte-identical artifact already in `onedoor/_vendor`"*
**Status:** not blocking. `ND-001` is built and chaining is off in every store that
matters. **`ND-010` proceeds** — this touches the preimage, not the rebuild.

---

## 1. What was checked

R032 §2 says the dialect is *"Not a resemblance: the same scheme, read from the
vendored files."* That is a factual claim about specific files, so it was checked
against them rather than assumed.

**`onedoor/_vendor/canonical.py`** — `sha256 = 98a50bc3f3f71e3ad1b2743f2b71db5cb37a33da1851d8d7d4d83578e865406f`,
byte-identical to `reference/rederivable-manifest/canonical.py` (asserted by
`tests/reference/`, and re-confirmed here).

**Every byte literal in the entire artifact**, all ten files, scanned
programmatically rather than by eye:

| File | Byte literals |
|---|---|
| `canonical.py` | `b""`, the 65536-chunk read sentinel, `b"\x00"`, `b"\x01"` |
| `instruments.py` | regex string fragments only |
| `validate.py` | `b"A"`, `b"B"`, `b"C"`, `b"forged"`, `b"other"` — self-test fixtures |

The two that matter are `_LEAF_PREFIX = b"\x00"` and `_NODE_PREFIX = b"\x01"`: RFC 6962
§2.1 domain separation for the Merkle tree.

**Absent from the vendored file**, checked by substring:

- `to_bytes` — **not present**
- `pack(` — **not present**
- `uid` — **not present**
- `big` / `little` (byte order) — **not present**
- `struct` — present **only** inside the English words *construction* and *structures*

## 2. What follows

There is **no uid-preimage convention and no length-prefix dialect anywhere in the
artifact**, in either copy. Its six frozen rules cover decimals, datetimes, strings,
JSON, digests and the RFC 6962 construction — a canonical-JSON scheme and a Merkle
scheme, neither of which frames a sequence of typed fields.

So the sentence *"read from the vendored files"* cannot be executed, and delivery is
not going to write it into `docs/row-preimage.md` as though it had been. That document
is now, by R032 §2's own ruling, the **single normative source** that `ND-015` and
`ND-017` cite and never re-derive. A normative source whose first section describes a
provenance that does not exist is worse than one that admits its encoding is new: the
second is checkable, the first quietly invites a future implementer to go looking for a
convention they will never find, and to guess when they cannot.

This is the second time the convention has been cited. The first time (`CONFORMANCE.md`
§5, after R031) delivery reported it and core responded by naming it more precisely
rather than by pointing at code. Delivery may be looking in the wrong place — but the
place was named explicitly this time, and it has been read exhaustively.

## 3. What delivery has done in the meantime

The parts of R032 §2 that **are** executable are done, because they are right and they
do not depend on this:

- **One length-prefix dialect programme-wide.** `docs/row-preimage.md` §2 defines
  exactly one, and it is the only one onedoor has. If `onewatch` or `onetrace` needs
  framing, this is the dialect to adopt — the goal of §2 is met, by a document rather
  than by a vendored module.
- **`docs/row-preimage.md` is the single normative source**, and it now says so.
  `tests/guardrail/test_row_preimage.py` enforces it structurally: `preimage.py` is
  the only module in `onedoor/` permitted to construct row-preimage bytes, so
  `ND-015`'s `sig` and `ND-017`'s `E` **cannot** re-derive without failing CI. X-14 at
  the preimage, guarded rather than promised.
- **The extension is documented as an extension**, built on the one byte-level
  discipline the artifact does ratify — rule 6's one-byte domain-separation tags.

## 4. What core can rule

1. **The artifact is the wrong place and the dialect lives elsewhere** — name the file
   and delivery re-vendors and re-encodes. **Cheap until a real store chains**, which
   has not happened: chaining is opt-in and off, and no deployment has enabled it.
2. **There is no prior dialect, and onedoor's is now the programme's** — then
   `docs/row-preimage.md` §1 is corrected to say so plainly, `onewatch` and `onetrace`
   adopt it by citation, and the sentence about a vendored convention is withdrawn.
3. **Something delivery has missed** — say where to look and it will be read.

Delivery's own reading is **(2)**, on the evidence. But the preimage is core's to
freeze, and stating a provenance that cannot be checked is the one thing this
programme's whole apparatus exists to prevent — so it is core's ruling, not delivery's
assumption.
